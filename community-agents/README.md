# Community Agents

These ten agents are stateless role instructions for manual, one-off use with a general-purpose language model. Copy one file into your tool, add your project brief and review the output before acting on it.

They are intentionally limited: they do not retain project state, select other roles, call external systems or coordinate a team automatically. For higher-quality work, provide evidence, constraints and a clear decision that the output should support.

## Suggested sequence

For a new product idea, a useful manual sequence is:

1. Market Researcher
2. User Researcher
3. Product Strategist
4. Risk Reviewer

For content or office projects, start with the relevant specialist and finish with the Risk Reviewer.

## Included agents

- Market Researcher — demand, competitors and differentiation
- User Researcher — segments, pain points and validation questions
- Product Strategist — positioning, MVP scope and validation
- Content Planner — content positioning, topics and weekly plans
- Office Process Analyst — workflows, SOPs and automation opportunities
- Risk Reviewer — evidence, logic, scope and major risks
- Brand Positioning Advisor — positioning, message hierarchy and creative direction
- Sales Message Advisor — outreach, discovery and follow-up drafts
- Customer Feedback Analyst — traceable themes and decision support
- Project Scope Reviewer — deliverables, dependencies and acceptance criteria

These files do not include automatic role selection, model routing, persistent memory, tool execution, team coordination or Skill evolution. Those operating capabilities belong to hosted ClawHire.
