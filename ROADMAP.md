# ClawHire public roadmap

This roadmap communicates direction, not guaranteed delivery dates. Priorities may change based on invite-test feedback.

## Available in the MVP

- Web workspace with research, content, product, office, commerce and custom project entry points.
- AI CEO discovery, initial diagnosis, project consensus and execution planning.
- Role-based work allocation and text/Markdown deliverables.
- Project views, approval entry points and a deliverable center.
- Multi-model routing, usage credits and subscription-plan foundations.

## Invite-test focus

- Small-scale invite testing and user interviews.
- Improve first-project and first-delivery success rates.
- Strengthen credit accounting, validation, failure recovery and monitoring.
- Connect production payments and subscription state.

## Later directions

- Web research with cited sources.
- File, spreadsheet and additional deliverable formats.
- Code execution and product-prototype delivery.
- Image, video and brand-asset generation.
- Workplace and publishing integrations.
- Team seats, permissions, audit and enterprise capabilities.
