# Sanitized example: AI recipe product validation

This fictional example illustrates a first-round ClawHire project. It does not contain customer data and is not evidence that every suggested market claim is true.

## Starting brief

> Explore an AI nutrition recipe assistant for young urban professionals who want healthier weekday meals but have limited time. Decide whether a small MVP is worth testing.

Constraints: two-week validation window, small budget, no medical diagnosis or individualized treatment claims.

## Project consensus

- **Decision:** whether to run a concierge-style MVP test.
- **Target user:** busy professionals who cook some weekday meals and struggle with planning.
- **Primary uncertainty:** whether meal-planning friction is painful enough to change behavior or pay.
- **Non-goal:** building a complete recommendation engine before validation.

## Role plan

| Role | Assignment | Expected output |
|---|---|---|
| Market Researcher | Map alternatives and differentiation hypotheses | Market validation memo |
| User Researcher | Design interviews around past behavior | Interview guide and evidence criteria |
| Product Strategist | Define the smallest credible test | MVP strategy brief |
| Risk Reviewer | Review claims, privacy and scope | Risk review and release conditions |

## Example first-round output

### MVP hypothesis

Users who already attempt weekday meal planning may value a service that turns stated constraints into a short, editable weekly plan and shopping list. This is a hypothesis to validate, not a proven market fact.

### Two-week test

1. Recruit 8–12 participants matching behavioral criteria.
2. Interview them about recent planning attempts, workarounds and abandoned meals.
3. Manually deliver a weekly plan to qualified participants.
4. Measure plan usage, edits, repeat requests and willingness to continue.
5. Review objections and failure cases before deciding what to build.

### Decision signals

- **Proceed:** repeated use and strong evidence that the plan replaces meaningful effort.
- **Revise:** interest exists, but trust, dietary constraints or editing friction dominates.
- **Stop:** polite intent is not matched by use, return behavior or meaningful problem evidence.

### Risk conditions

- Avoid diagnosis, treatment or guaranteed health outcomes.
- Collect only data needed for the test and provide a deletion path.
- Require human review for allergy-related and other high-consequence constraints.
- Cite and verify any externally researched claims before publication.
