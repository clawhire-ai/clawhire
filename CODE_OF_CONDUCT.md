# Community code of conduct

We want discussions and contributions to be useful, respectful and safe.

- Critique ideas and artifacts, not people.
- Be specific, evidence-aware and open about uncertainty.
- Do not publish personal data, credentials, confidential information or content you do not have permission to share.
- Do not use issues or discussions for spam, impersonation, harassment or misleading promotion.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. For private concerns, email [service@clawhireai.com](mailto:service@clawhireai.com).
